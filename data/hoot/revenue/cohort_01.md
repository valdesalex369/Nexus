# Nova Agency — Outreach Cohort 01

## Objective
Convert the completed 25-prospect discovery set into a falsifiable first revenue experiment. Prospect accumulation is frozen until conversion evidence shows volume is the constraint.

## Success criteria
1. Rank exactly five prospects for first human outreach.
2. Use explicit scoring instead of intuition alone.
3. Give each prospect one evidence-grounded wedge and one decision-maker target.
4. Define a minimal funnel so outcomes can be measured.
5. Do not claim unverified marketing deficiencies.

## Scoring
Each dimension is 1–5. Weighted score = 40% closeability + 35% economic value + 25% owner accessibility.

## Why these five
The cohort intentionally favors owner-accessible local operators with documented high-ticket work or multiple valuable service classes. It avoids selecting solely by review count or business size. The experiment needs conversations with people who can say yes, not merely impressive company names.

## Measurement baseline
Record one row per outreach attempt with:
- prospect
- date/time
- channel
- decision maker reached: yes/no
- meaningful conversation: yes/no
- audit requested/accepted: yes/no
- meeting booked: yes/no
- proposal requested/sent: yes/no
- closed revenue: dollars
- rejection reason
- next action/date

Primary funnel: attempts → decision-maker conversations → audits → meetings → proposals → closed dollars.

## Falsification rule
Do not add prospect #26 until at least one of these is true:
- the first cohort has been attempted and fewer than two decision-maker conversations were reached because contact quality was poor; or
- conversion data otherwise demonstrates prospect volume/quality is the active constraint.

If conversations occur but audits/meetings do not, fix the offer or opening. If meetings occur but proposals do not, fix diagnosis/value articulation. If proposals occur but deals do not, investigate price, trust, proof, or offer risk.
